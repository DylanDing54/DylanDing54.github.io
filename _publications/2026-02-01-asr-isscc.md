**Highlight paper.**

This work presents a CIM-based streaming multi-speaker ASR accelerator featuring context-aware redundancy skipping, a 2D-writable MX-DCIM with static and dynamic pages, and a similarity-aware TCAM for approximate speaker search and N-best caching.

As part of this project I independently implemented an end-to-end real-time KWS demo system, covering the entire silicon lifecycle — from RTL design and FPGA prototyping to backend physical implementation and successful tape-out. Post-silicon functional validation integrated MCU-based VAD and MFCC preprocessing with a designed PCB. A lightweight CNN trained on GSCD and quantized via PTQ runs on the CIM macro and a custom VPU (im2col, pooling) under RISC-V CPU control.
