ChipLight develops a cross-layer optimization framework for LLM training clusters built on chiplets and optical interconnects. Evaluation shows that LLM training traffic is uneven in both space and time, so optical links and OCS reconfiguration can be used more efficiently through topology optimization and dynamic link reuse.

The proposed framework jointly optimizes MCM architecture, optical network topology, and training parallelism to reduce communication bottlenecks, with a cost-aware model included. The resulting architecture achieves up to 19.58x higher training throughput than conventional GPU clusters and a 41% improvement over the prior network design at similar cost.
